Research Collaborations

Designing, executing, and transparently evaluating experiments across AI safety and security.

For people with a question and a dataset, or a half-finished experiment, who want a careful collaborator. I care about reading results honestly, including the negative ones.

What we can work on

Choosing datasets and baselines

Picking data and comparison points that actually test your claim.

Designing experiments

Setting up evaluations with cross-validation and hypothesis testing, so results can be trusted.

Reading results honestly

Separating what worked from what only moved the problem around, and reporting partial or negative results plainly.

Working as a co-researcher

Running evaluations, building prototypes and contributing figures and writing, as I have on papers in intrusion detection, adversarial ML and federated learning.

How it works

  1. Send me the question and what you have so far (data, code, draft results).
  2. We agree on the scope and who does what.
  3. We run it, check it, and write up what we find.
