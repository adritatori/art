How I can support you

Research Mentorship

Turning vague ideas into rigorous, answerable research questions and executable plans.

For students and early researchers who want to do real research. I don't hand out topics. I help you think: what is the real problem, why does it matter, and what evidence would change your mind?

What we can work on

Finding your research question

Turning an observation or interest into a focused, answerable question, and spotting real gaps in the literature.

Understanding the problem deeply

Thinking critically about a topic: the hidden assumptions, what has been tried, and why it fell short.

Scoping a thesis or paper

Deciding what is worth doing, and what to leave out, so the work is finishable.

Impact so far

Students I mentored have published their first research papers at international conferences, on adversarial detection, dataset coverage with multi-LLM consensus, and healthcare cyber-threat modelling.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your interest or topic, and where you feel stuck.
  2. We agree on what you want to get out of it.
  3. We work through it together, with honest feedback.
