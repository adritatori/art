Academic Writing & Submissions

Structuring manuscripts, figures, target venue selection, and reviewer response strategies.

For people writing their first paper, or stuck on a draft. Writing is where good work is often lost, so I help make the argument clear.

What we can work on

Structure and framing

What the paper claims, in what order, and what to cut.

Figures and tables

Making results readable at a glance, with Matplotlib, Seaborn and LaTeX tooling.

Picking a venue

Choosing a conference or journal that fits the work.

Responding to reviewers

Reading reviews calmly, deciding what to change, and writing a clear reply.

How it works

  1. Send me your draft or outline, and the venue you have in mind.
  2. I give you honest feedback on the argument first, the wording second.
  3. We revise until it is ready to submit.
